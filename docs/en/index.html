About me

Passionate about technology, self-taught by conviction

I have been building for Android for more than 5 years. I learned on my own, by building and shipping: every app in this portfolio came from a problem in front of me that nobody had solved the way I wanted.

I like software that gets to the point: opens fast, does not ask for permissions it does not need, and does what it promises. If one of my apps is useful to you, a review on Google Play goes a long way toward keeping them improving.

I work with  Android · Kotlin · Java · Jetpack Compose · Material Design · SQLite / Room · Firebase · Google Play Console

  • Simple before complete

    I would rather ship an app that does three things well than one that does thirty halfway.

  • Your data is yours

    I request the minimum permissions and spell out in every policy what is stored and where.

  • Built for real phones

    I optimize for mid-range devices, which is what most of us actually carry.
